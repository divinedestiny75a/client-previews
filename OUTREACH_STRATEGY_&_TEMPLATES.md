## 🎯 The Secret Angle: "I Already Built It For You"
Traditional cold outreach says: *"Hey, do you want to hire me to build a website for \$1,500?"* (Response rate: < 1%).
Our strategy uses the **"Live Prototype Hook"**:
> *"Hey [Name], I noticed you didn't have an official website listed on the Chamber of Commerce directory, so I went ahead and drafted a working preview for [Business Name] to see what you think. You can test it here: [Link]. No obligation at all."*

**Why this converts at 15–25%:**
1. Zero guesswork: They can see their own name, phone number, and services rendered in modern, sleek design on their phone screen.
2. Loss aversion: It already exists, and seeing it makes them realize how much better it looks than just an unverified listing.
3. Ridiculously low friction pricing: Under \$299 or \$199 + \$29/mo hosting.
